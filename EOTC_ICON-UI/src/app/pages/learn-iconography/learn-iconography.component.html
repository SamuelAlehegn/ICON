Learn About Iconography in Christianity

An introduction to the theology, history, and tradition of Christian iconography, with a particular focus on the Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church.

What is iconography?

Christian icons are not merely decorative art; they are windows into the heavenly realm. In Orthodox tradition, icons are venerated as theological texts in color, conveying the gospel through imagery rather than words alone.

The Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church has its own rich, distinct tradition of icon painting, with unique styles, palettes, and local saintly traditions developed over centuries.

Key themes

  • Theology of the image: The veneration of icons flows from the Incarnation — God became visible in Christ.
  • Symbolism: Colors, gestures, halos, inscriptions, and composition carry theological meaning.
  • Tradition: Iconographers often follow established prototypes handed down across generations.
  • Ethiopian distinctives: Vibrant colors, elongated forms, local saints, and unique narrative compositions.
